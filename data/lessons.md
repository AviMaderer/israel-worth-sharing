# Lessons from missed stories

The task reads this before every run and applies each lesson. Each lesson is added after Avi sends a story the search had missed. Edit or delete lines freely.

- Check hospital announcements every run (Shaare Zedek, Sheba, Hadassah, Rambam, Ichilov, Beilinson): search "first in Israel" plus the hospital names, and read outlets that carry hospital press releases (Arutz Sheva / israelnationalnews.com, VIN News).
- Read hospitals' own newsrooms, not just news outlets: e.g. hadassah.org/story ("Medicine & Research"), Sheba, Shaare Zedek and Clalit press pages. "First patient in Israel" stories often appear there first.
- Check Israeli-innovation newsletters and Substacks (e.g. sharingfromisrael.substack.com) for medtech startups founded by Israeli doctors; such stories are acceptable when they report real milestones (successful animal trials, approved human trials), stated factually.
- When foreign news reports a medical device "first in the US/Europe", check who developed it: US local coverage often omits that the company is Israeli. Search "Israeli-developed" and check the company in the Israel Innovation Authority's listings.
